# AI-Based Road Maintenance Priority Optimizer

A Civil Engineering + recommendation + optimization teaching project. An MLP predicts road-level maintenance benefit and an exact 0/1 knapsack optimizer selects the highest-benefit affordable programme.

The benefit weights are educational policy assumptions. Real programmes require validated condition surveys, treatment alternatives, lifecycle cost, legal duties, safety, accessibility, geographic equity, stakeholder input, finance constraints, accountable approval, and uncertainty analysis.

Files include `Road_Maintenance_Priority_Optimizer.ipynb`, Streamlit `app.py`, `bridge.py`, `story.py`, 32-scene `illustration_gallery.py`, and `tools/build_notebook.py`. Replace `https://road-maintenance-priority.streamlit.app` after deployment and rebuild.
