## Style Prompt

Semi-realistic technical training imagery with physically believable computer equipment, restrained cinematic lighting, clear instructional overlays, and smooth motion that follows real data paths.

## Colors

- Background: #06111d
- Foreground: #eef8ff
- Data movement: #ffd166
- Active components: #5ee5aa
- Output and connection: #43d9ff

## Typography

- Interface and captions: DM Sans
- Headings: Space Grotesk

## What NOT to Do

- Do not represent the main equipment as generic cards or boxes.
- Do not use floating futuristic holograms.
- Do not overload the illustration with labels.
- Do not animate without explaining the movement in a caption.
