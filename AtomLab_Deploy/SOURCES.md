# Teaching scope and references

This app teaches atomic structure as foundation/revision for a Class 10 learner. It does not claim that these 14 sections are the official Class 10 chapter list. Atomic-structure material has historically appeared in Class 9 science, and NCERT editions and chapter numbering are changing; consult the learner's school textbook.

- [NCERT textbook library](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php) — use the edition assigned by the school.
- [NCERT Grade 9 secondary syllabus](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/announcement/Syllabus_Secondary_Stage_Phase_I_Grade_9_Part_2.pdf) — atomic-structure learning outcomes.
- [CBSE archived Class IX Science assessment manual](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/publication/archive/CBSE-FA-Class-IX%20%28Science%29%20Final.pdf) — includes Structure of the Atom activities and assessment context.
- [Groq supported models](https://console.groq.com/docs/models) — tutor provider configuration.

Lessons and diagrams are original explanations of standard school chemistry. No textbook illustrations were copied. The tutor uses the local lesson notes; it does not scrape or search the web during a reply.
