Follow the blood

The particles represent blood. Both sides of the heart work together.

Blue · To the lungs

Oxygen-poor blood returns from the body through the venae cavae.

Right atrium → tricuspid valve → right ventricle

The right ventricle pumps it through the pulmonary valve and pulmonary artery to the lungs.

Red · To the body

In the lungs, blood picks up oxygen and releases carbon dioxide. It returns through the pulmonary veins.

Left atrium → mitral valve → left ventricle

The left ventricle pumps it through the aortic valve and aorta to the body.

Valves prevent backflow. Blue means oxygen-poor; real blood is always red. Lungs and body are outside this view. The particle paths are a teaching guide, not an exact simulation of the artist’s animation.
