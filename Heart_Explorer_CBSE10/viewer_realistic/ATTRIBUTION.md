Downloaded from the public redistribution at:
https://github.com/carolinacherry/cardia-survey/blob/main/assets/heart.glb
The GLB embeds the original author, original source URL, title and CC-BY-4.0 license.

HeartLab additions: local viewer, lighting, normalization, approximate exterior study labels, tutor topic buttons and illustrative surface contraction. Original mesh and textures retained. This model has no internal cutaway or native animation. Its added surface motion does not simulate cardiac physiology. Double-click toggles to the separate simplified teaching anatomy, bundled as teaching-heart.glb. The original exterior has not been cut or supplied with scan-derived internal anatomy.

Three.js runtime is copied from the existing viewer; see its bundled license.

## Artist-made animated interior

Beating Heart by Dreamwasabducted; remix by jalmer, CC BY 4.0. Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/beating-heart-d9845afb1ee64ad094adc96320c67d98 . License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . User-supplied archive: beating-heart.zip.

Original skinned geometry and one-second skeletal animation retained. Material converted from specular/glossiness to metallic/roughness; emissive texture disabled; blue diffuse regions tinted toward tissue in the viewer shader. Labels are approximate study markers; the source is one skinned mesh. Flow and phase notes are schematic overlays, not calibrated to the artist animation. Legacy teaching model retained.
